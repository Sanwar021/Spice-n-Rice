## Supplied Figma design assets

These are the exact illustrative food photographs referenced by `Restaurant Website UI Design.zip`, re-encoded locally as WebP. They are not represented as photographs of this restaurant's actual dishes.

- `design/food-0.webp`: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1631515243349-e0cb75fb8d3a
- `design/food-1.webp`: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1603894584373-5ac82b2ae398

- `../fonts/design-font-*.woff2`: the reference's DM Sans and Fraunces Latin font faces, self-hosted from the Google Fonts stylesheets referenced by the export.

The ZIP's prototype admin/localStorage data was not used as the production backend. All current menu and catering prices come from PostgreSQL.
